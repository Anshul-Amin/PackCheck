# Dancing Fish Brand Packaging Guidelines & QA Policy (North America)
*Internal Regulatory & Quality Assurance Standard - Rev 3.2*

## Section 1: Brand Identity & Graphic Requirements
1. **Brand Name Display**: The brand name "DANCING FISH" must appear prominently on the Principal Display Panel (PDP) and the top flap.
2. **Statement of Identity**: The product descriptor ("GRANOLA - BERRY CRUNCH") must be placed on the PDP in legible typography immediately following or adjacent to the brand mark.

## Section 2: Copy Approval & Content Sheet Consistency
3. **Word-for-Word Fidelity**: All marketing claims, storage statements, and dietary descriptions printed on artwork must match the approved content sheet word-for-word without unapproved editorial deviations.
4. **No Unauthorized Disease or Structure/Function Claims**: No explicit or implied claims that the food treats, cures, or mitigates any disease are permitted unless authorized by FDA health claim regulations.
5. **Net Weight Presentation**: Net quantity must reflect the approved production batch fill size and include dual metric and imperial designations for all North American retail cartons.

## Section 3: Barcode and Traceability Standards
6. **UPC-A Compliance**: All retail cartons must feature a high-contrast GS1-compliant UPC-A barcode with a valid check digit calculated according to the GS1 Modulo-10 algorithm. The printed digits must strictly match the Content Sheet UPC field (`1 20000 00003 6`).

## Section 4: Allergens and Advisory Labeling
7. **Allergen Transparency Policy**: If any of the 9 major food allergens (including tree nuts like almonds, pecans, walnuts) are formulation ingredients, they must be affirmatively stated in an allergen declaration statement. Advisory "May Contain" wording cannot replace an ingredient allergen declaration.

## Section 5: Environmental and Certifications
8. **Recyclability Designation**: Cartons should carry an approved recycling logo (e.g. How2Recycle paperboard symbol) on an informational panel.
9. **Third-Party Certifications**: Artwork carrying certification marks such as "Certified Vegan" or "USDA Organic" must strictly ensure the ingredient formulation contains no prohibited substances (such as honey in vegan certified products).
